Translate Discord DMs: why they start off, how to turn one on

Updated 2026-11-16

Subline reads server channels by itself, but direct messages and group DMs stay off until you choose. Here is why, and how to turn one conversation on.

The short answer

Subline does not translate your direct messages by itself. Server channels are on from the start. Direct messages and group DMs are off until you turn one on, one conversation at a time.

To turn one on, open the DM, hover any message, press the 🌐 button, and choose Enable auto-translate here. From then on, that conversation gets a short translated line under each message, marked ✦. Only you see it.

Why DMs start off

A server channel is a public room. A DM is a private talk between you and one friend, or a few. Those are two different kinds of trust.

When Subline translates a message, the text has to be sent to be translated. In a server channel you are reading a room that many people share. In a DM, you may not want a friend's private words to go anywhere until you have decided that yourself. So the choice is yours, per conversation.

The privacy page lists exactly what is sent and what stays on your computer. Read it before you turn a DM on.

Turn one DM on, step by step

  1. Open the DM or group DM in Discord.
  2. Hover any message in it.
  3. Press the 🌐 button.
  4. Choose Enable auto-translate here.

The same button now says Disable auto-translate here. Press it again to turn the conversation off. Nothing else changes, and no other DM is affected.

A DM you might turn on

Say a friend you met in a Spanish-speaking study server writes to you in private:

  • marta: oye, ¿te acuerdas del ejercicio de ayer? no lo entendí bien
  • marta: si tienes un rato me lo explicas por llamada
  • marta: gracias, eres un crack

With the DM on, you see under each line: "hey, do you remember yesterday's exercise? I didn't really get it", then "if you have a minute, explain it to me on a call", then "thanks, you're a star". Marta sees her own messages and nothing more. Your reply is yours to write in your own words.

What stays the same in a DM

  • Messages already in your language get no line.
  • Laughter, emoji and links get no line.
  • Your own messages are not translated. Subline reads for you. It does not write for you.
  • A friend's name or bio in another script can still be read on hover.

A few things work even when the DM is off. Bios and custom statuses in the DM list and Friends are translated, because they are profile text, not your private talk. The privacy page's table says this too.

Questions people ask

Can I turn on all my DMs at once? The button works per conversation. Turn on the ones you want.

Does the other person know? No. Subline adds nothing to the chat and sends no message. Only your screen shows the line.

What about voice calls? Subline does not translate voice. It reads text only.

Does it work on my phone? Not yet. It runs in the Discord app on Mac and Windows, and in Discord in Chrome.

One thing to know first

Subline is built on Vencord, a client mod, and Discord's terms do not allow client mods. The safety page says this plainly, and explains what Subline does and never does. Subline is not made by or affiliated with Discord.
